# I'm Tim Zgeybi

**Founder & CEO at [Kapichu](https://kapichu.ru) · Full-stack developer**

I'm building **Kapichu**, a platform that combines machine-learning forecasts, advertising analytics, and automated bid management for Wildberries sellers. Our focus is advertising profitability, accounting for product margins rather than clicks alone.

I lead the company and stay hands-on with the code. My background is in full-stack web development and teaching programming.

## 🚀 What I'm working on

- Building Kapichu's seller platform: Flask APIs, background workers, and the React frontend.
- Connecting Wildberries data with forecasting and automated bidding workflows.
- Working directly with sellers, developing the product, and growing the team.

## 🎓 Education

I graduated from the **Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)** with a **bachelor's degree in Computer Science**. I'm now studying **IT Entrepreneurship at MIPT**.
