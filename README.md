## Currently building: Kapichu

Kapichu connects marketplace data, performance forecasts, and bidding decisions. Our goal is to improve advertising profitability by accounting for product margins and predicted performance, rather than optimizing for clicks or ad spend alone.

My current focus:

- **Product and engineering:** building the seller platform, from backend services and background jobs to the web interface.
- **Data and automation:** integrating Wildberries APIs with forecasting and automated bidding workflows.
- **Building the business:** working with sellers, shaping the product around their needs, and growing the team.
